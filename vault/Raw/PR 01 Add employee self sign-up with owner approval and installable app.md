## Summary
- **Self sign-up:** the sign-in screen has a "Create an account" button, and a shareable `/?signup=1` link opens the sign-up form directly. Employees enter their name, email and password, then confirm their email.
- **Waiting for approval:** a signed-up employee who isn't a member yet automatically submits an access request. They see a "Waiting for approval" screen with a "Check again" button and no business data.
- **Owner approval queue:** the owner gets an "Access requests (N)" button with a "Copy sign-up link" action. Each request has a role picker plus Approve and Decline. Approval ties access to that employee's verified Supabase user ID, so no email is sent. Each decision is written to the audit log.
- **Security:** only the owner can approve or decline. Signing up alone never grants access. Declined or removed employees can't re-request their way back in, though the owner can still invite them by email. Pending requests are hidden from anyone who isn't an owner or administrator.
- **Phone install:** adds a web app manifest, icons and theme/apple meta tags, so employees can add Rigo to their home screen and it opens full screen.
- Updated `DEPLOYMENT.md` with the Supabase settings this needs (email sign-ups on, email confirmation on, `https://YOUR-DOMAIN/` in the redirect allowlist).
