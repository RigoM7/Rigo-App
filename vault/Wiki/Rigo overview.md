# Rigo overview

Rigo is a configurable business management and automation platform for **field-service companies**. An owner creates a company workspace, invites the team, configures services and workflows, and picks how much Rigo automates, while keeping control through approvals, pause and takeover.

**First industries:** fuel delivery, portable toilet delivery/rental/servicing/pickup, and septic services. One company can offer several; all services share the same customers, service locations, employees, trucks and equipment. Other industries come later through templates and configuration, not code.

**What Rigo takes on:** preparing invoices, notifying people, drafting follow-ups and customer communications, planning recurring visits.

**Live site:** https://rigo-app-dun.vercel.app

**Business model:** creating a real company is free. Monthly charging is a future direction; no price is chosen and no billing exists. See [[Open questions]].
